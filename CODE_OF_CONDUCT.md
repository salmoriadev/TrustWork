# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We are committed to a harassment-free, welcoming community. Be respectful, accept constructive feedback, and focus discussion on the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable.

Project maintainers may remove content or participation that violates these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Enforcement decisions will prioritize safety, context, and proportionality.
